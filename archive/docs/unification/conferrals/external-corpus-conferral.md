## Evidence as put

- **The binding constraint is labelled rows** (`GD-28`): a two-cell split of the 70–90 band at
  BF ≥ 10 needs ≈385 verdicted band rows, the owner supplies ≈7 a month, and the Claude verdict
  channel has been dormant since 2026-07-22. proplang#26 rests on 238 ticks / 141 questions.
- **The owner's survey of utility conventions** (pasted into the session from a parallel
  research session): the gauge is the standard reject-option form (Chow's rule); Kalai et al.
  (2025) give the explicit-threshold penalty `t/(1−t)`, so the elicited −9 is the `t = 0.9`
  convention; the literature's own answer is that the number is a per-application policy
  choice. Read against the register: OQ-0 (the regime) was already ruled (`M-34`/`GD-29`); the
  *value* is an identified latent (`GD-27`) and therefore evidence, not convention — and
  evidence is exactly what the supply constraint withholds.
- **A public labelled corpus exists** (ATM-Bench, arXiv 2603.01990; data CC-BY-NC 4.0, code
  MIT): 6,741 anonymised emails, 1,013 + 25 QA with human-annotated evidence ids. Read by the
  existing harness over a second KB root it grows the verdict supply ~10× at single-digit
  dollars, with no PII exposure to the public repo.

   replace evidence with a convention on a quantity the register classifies as a latent.
