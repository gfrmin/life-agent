# Work & income — Ada Lovelace

Synthetic record of how the demo owner earns money.

Ada is **self-employed** — an independent contractor, not an employee. Her
registered trading status is "Self-Employed / Independent Contractor".

Main client and sources of income:

- **Analytical Engine Works** — a retainer of 4,200 per month for mathematical
  consulting. Invoiced on the first of each month, paid net-30.
- **Bernoulli Tutoring** — occasional teaching, billed per session at 75 each.
- Royalties on the translated "Notes" — paid twice a year.

Ada raises invoices under the name *A. Lovelace Consulting*. She sets aside a
third of every payment for income tax, which she files annually as a sole
trader. She has no salaried employer and no monthly payslip.
